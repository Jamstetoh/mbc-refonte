About MBC

A Center Built on One Conviction: Science Must Serve Communities

In developing countries, the gap between health problems and scientific solutions is wide and deep — and research receives little support. Created to change that, the Molecular Biology Center brings together a specialized, multidisciplinary team — creative, innovative, dedicated and hard-working — to develop scientific research in the growing field of biotechnology.

Training & Workshops

Strengthen the field of Molecular Biology through hands-on training and workshops for students and professionals.

Applied Research

Promote biotechnology and applied research that answers real community health needs.

Discovery

Discover new restriction enzymes — including our patented CspCI — recognized and commercialized worldwide.

Diagnostics & Products

Develop affordable diagnostic tools and molecular biology products for African laboratories.
