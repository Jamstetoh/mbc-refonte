Founder

Dr. Celine Nkenfou

Parasitology, molecular biology

Dr. Celine Nkenfou founded the Molecular Biology Center in 1996. A pioneering Cameroonian molecular biologist, she has built a career spanning parasitology, molecular diagnostics and biotechnology capacity-building — shaping biomedical science across Central Africa.

Her doctoral work on restriction endonucleases was conducted under Dr. Richard Roberts, Nobel Laureate in Physiology or Medicine (1993), followed by a postdoctoral position as visiting scientist at the Novartis Institute for Tropical Diseases.
