[SECTION 03 - A PROPOS]
Overline : About MBC
Titre : A Center Built on One Conviction: Science Must Serve Communities
Texte : In developing countries, the gap between health problems and
scientific solutions is wide and deep - and research receives little
support. Created to change that, the Molecular Biology Center brings
together a specialized, multidisciplinary team - creative, innovative,
dedicated and hard-working - to develop scientific research in the
growing field of biotechnology.
Nos 4 missions (puces) :
 - Strengthen the field of Molecular Biology through training and workshops
 - Promote biotechnology and applied research
 - Discover new restriction enzymes
 - Develop affordable diagnostic tools and molecular biology products
Bouton : Learn More About Us

[SECTION 04 - RECHERCHE]
Overline : Our Research
Titre : Science With Real-World Impact
Carte 1 - Tropical Diseases : Diagnosis and epidemiology of malaria,
tuberculosis and dengue fever, plus drug discovery from Cameroon's plant
biodiversity - including plant extracts with proven anti-HIV activity
in cell culture.
Carte 2 - Restriction Enzymes : Discovery and characterization of new
restriction enzymes, including the patented CspCI. Seven of our enzymes
are commercialized worldwide by New England Biolabs (USA).
Carte 3 - Local Scientific Manufacturing : Reducing the cost of basic
laboratory equipment through local manufacturing and maintenance
capacity - so that every African laboratory can thrive.
Bandeau highlight : Cameroon Bacterial Type Collection - 2,000 isolates
recorded and growing.
Bouton : Discover Our Research
