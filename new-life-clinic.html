Our approach

A sanctuary of global health, adapted to urban life

New Life Clinic is not only a curative care center. It is a place where prevention, listening and modern organization come together for your well-being.

Clinical excellence and active prevention

Health is won upstream: early screening, personalized executive check-ups and rigorous follow-up of chronic diseases such as hypertension and diabetes.

Humanized care and listening

Every patient, from young mothers to seniors, feels listened to, respected and accompanied with unwavering kindness at every step of the care journey.

Organizational innovation

Modern management methods, digital accessibility and responsiveness adapted to the demands of the capital, inspired by international public health research.

Our services

Four poles of care for the whole family

Explore our departments and book the service that fits your needs in one tap.

Consultations & General Care

Everyday medicine for the whole family, with continuity and rigor.

  • General medicine and routine consultations: care for common conditions for children, adults and seniors.
  • Proximity emergencies and continuous care: rapid management of minor trauma and light medical emergencies.
  • Chronic disease follow-up (diabetes, hypertension): dedicated support programs with a community-based, integrated approach.

Mother & Child Health

A reassuring journey from pregnancy to childhood.

  • Prenatal and postnatal consultations: personalized support for expectant and new mothers.
  • Pediatric follow-up and vaccination: growth monitoring and respect for the vaccination schedule in a caring setting.
  • Family planning and reproductive health: tailored and confidential counseling for every stage of life.

Prevention, Well-being & Innovation

What makes the clinic unique: preventing rather than curing.

  • Preventive health check-ups: screening packages adapted to age and sex (diabetes, hypertension, female cancers).
  • Therapeutic education and community workshops: sessions on lifestyle, nutrition and stress management.
  • Psychological support and listening: a mental health dimension integrated into whole-person care.

Exams & Laboratory

Fast, reliable results, explained with care.

  • Rapid diagnostic tests: malaria, HIV and blood glucose with results in minutes.
  • On-site sampling: collections processed with the scientific rigor of the Molecular Biology Center.
  • Clear result delivery: secure transmission with a medical explanation you can understand.

Medical Director

Dr. Nkenfou Pagnou Kelvine

General practitioner, Master in Public Health

An exceptional general practitioner committed to health equity, Dr. Nkenfou Pagnou Kelvine embodies the new generation of African medical leaders who combine scientific rigor, field innovation and deep human empathy. A graduate in medicine from the Catholic University of Central Africa with a Master's degree in Public Health, she leads the New Life Clinic with a forward-thinking vision.

Having honed her clinical skills in district and university hospitals across Cameroon, she has acquired solid mastery of hospital management, primary health care and comprehensive patient care. Her leadership was recognized through her election as best female staff member at the Baham District Hospital, and through her responsibilities within Women in Global Health Cameroon, the African Leaders Malaria Alliance and leading research networks.

Under her guidance, the New Life Clinic positions itself not only as a curative care center, but as a true sanctuary of global health adapted to the urban rhythm of Yaoundé, built around clinical excellence, humanized care and organizational innovation.
