Our Team

The people behind the science

A multidisciplinary team of scientists and advisors, supported by a Board of Directors: CEO, Executive Director, Finance Administrator and Legal Advisor.

Founder

Pr. Céline Nkenfou

Parasitology, molecular biology

Pr. Céline Nkenfou founded the Molecular Biology Center in 1996. A pioneering Cameroonian molecular biologist, she has built a career spanning parasitology, molecular diagnostics and biotechnology capacity-building, shaping biomedical science across Central Africa.

Her doctoral work on restriction endonucleases was conducted under Dr. Richard Roberts, Nobel Laureate in Physiology or Medicine (1993), followed by a postdoctoral position as visiting scientist at the Novartis Institute for Tropical Diseases.

Pr. Alex Matter

Director, Experimental Therapeutic Centre (ETC). Co-founder & CEO, Esperanza Medicines Foundation.

A pioneer of translational medicine in Asia, Pr. Matter has led groundbreaking drug discovery programs for neglected infectious diseases, including dengue and malaria. He advises MBC on therapeutic innovation and international research partnerships.

Pr. Subhash Vasudevan

Professor, Program in Emerging Infectious Diseases, Duke-NUS Medical School.

World-renowned for his work on dengue virus therapeutics and antiviral drug development, Pr. Vasudevan collaborates with MBC on emerging infectious diseases research and capacity building in molecular virology.
